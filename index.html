FAQ

Questions, answered.

How often should I clean my trash bin?

Monthly service is ideal for households that want routine cleaning and odor control. Quarterly service is a lower-frequency option.

Do I need to have my bin empty?

Yes. Your bin should be emptied after collection so it is ready for cleaning.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. As long as we can safely access the bin and your service area, you generally do not need to be home.

What if my pickup schedule changes?

Contact BinVanta as soon as possible so your cleaning visit can be coordinated with your collection schedule.
